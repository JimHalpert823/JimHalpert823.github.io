How to play

Every word is a quick mental workout. You have two answers to solve before the timer stops.

1

Give every letter a value

Q, W, E, R, T and Y are worth +1. Every other letter is worth −1. Add them up for the word's total score.

2

Cancel as you read

Work from left to right. Whenever opposite values meet, cancel the pair. Keep the surviving letters in their original order.

3

Beat the clock

Submit the total and remaining letters. Earn points for each correct answer, plus a speed bonus for a fast finish.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Which letters are worth +1?

Only Q, W, E, R, T and Y are +1. All other letters are −1.

How does letter cancellation work?

Read the word from left to right. Opposite values cancel each other; the remaining letters stay in their original order.

How are points awarded?

You get 10 points for the correct numerical score, 10 for the correct remaining letters, and a speed bonus of up to 10 points. The speed bonus is awarded only when both answers are correct.

Do I need to create an account?

No. This first version runs in your browser and does not require an account.

Are leaderboards and saved scores available?

Not yet. This launch version tracks your score and streak during your current game session. Online accounts, cross-device saves and leaderboards are planned for a later release.
